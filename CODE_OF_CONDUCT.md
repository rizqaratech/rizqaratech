# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

RizQara Tech is committed to maintaining a professional, respectful, and collaborative environment for team members, contributors, clients, and partners.

We believe good software is built through clear communication, respect, responsibility, and teamwork.

---

## Expected Behavior

All team members and contributors are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully.
- Be honest about progress, issues, and blockers.
- Respect client privacy and company confidentiality.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Work with accountability and discipline.
- Support team members when possible.
- Follow project guidelines and deadlines.
- Keep technical discussions focused and constructive.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insult, bullying, or personal attack
- Discrimination or disrespectful language
- Sharing private client or company data without permission
- Taking company leads, projects, or clients privately
- Uploading confidential code or credentials publicly
- Making false promises to clients
- Misrepresenting RizQara Tech services or pricing
- Spamming clients or using unprofessional communication
- Damaging the company reputation intentionally

---

## Client Communication Standard

When communicating with clients, team members should:

- Be professional and polite.
- Avoid fake guarantees.
- Never promise delivery without approval from the project lead.
- Never share internal pricing or private strategy unless allowed.
- Use official RizQara Tech documents, proposals, invoices, and agreements.
- Keep records of important client discussions.

---

## Team Communication Standard

Inside the team:

- Share updates clearly.
- Ask questions early.
- Report problems quickly.
- Respect decisions from project managers and team leads.
- Avoid hiding mistakes; fix them professionally.

---

## Reporting Issues

If you see behavior that breaks this Code of Conduct, contact the project manager or RizQara Tech management.

Email: rizqaratech@gmail.com

Reports will be reviewed carefully and handled professionally.

---

## Enforcement

Depending on the situation, RizQara Tech may take actions such as:

- Warning
- Task restriction
- Repository access removal
- Project removal
- Partnership termination
- Legal or contractual action for serious violations

---

## Our Standard

RizQara Tech expects every team member and collaborator to represent the company with professionalism, honesty, and respect.

**Build smart. Work responsibly. Grow together.**
